<p><strong>Anti-Bullying Policy</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong>
</p>
<p><strong><u>Our Commitment</u></strong>
</p>
<p>At Hong Wen School, we believe every child deserves to feel safe, valued,
competent and empowered in their learning journey. We are committed to
creating a caring and enabling school environment where all students can
flourish and develop positive relationships with their peers.</p>
<p>Our approach focuses on teaching kindness, empathy, and respect whilst
addressing any hurtful behaviour that may arise. We work together as a
school community – staff, students, and parents – to ensure everyone feels
they belong.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong><u>What is Bullying?</u></strong>
</p>
<p>Bullying involves persistent and intentional hurtful acts, while other
hurtful behaviours include once-off insensitive comments and physical fights.</p>
<p>Bullying and hurtful behaviours can take various forms, including verbal,
social, physical and cyber:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Verbal: Name-calling, spreading rumours, or making threats</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Social exclusion: Deliberately leaving someone out, or encouraging others
to ignore someone</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Physical actions: Pushing, hitting, or damaging someone's belongings</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Cyber bullying: Using online technology like phones, computers, or social
media to hurt or embarrass others on purpose and repeatedly</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong><u>The school’s firm stance against hurtful behaviours</u></strong>
</p>
<p><strong>The school takes a firm stance against all forms of hurtful behaviours</strong>,
from once-off, insensitive comments to persistent intentional acts of bullying
and physical violence. This stance guides our school in managing different
forms of hurtful behaviours.</p>
<p>We recognise that children may have disagreements or say things they do
not mean. We help students understand the difference between occasional
conflicts (which are normal) and persistent bullying behaviour, addressing
each situation with care and appropriate support.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong><u>How We Prevent Incidents</u></strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Take a firm stance against hurtful behaviours and bullying</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Foster a caring and enabling school environment, with positive teacher-student
relationships and strong peer support structure.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Conduct Character &amp; Citizenship Education lessons on empathy, conflict
management, resilience and cyber wellness</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Have regular communications with staff and students</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p><strong><u>How to Report Concerns</u></strong>
</p>
<p>We want parents and students to feel comfortable coming to us with any
concerns. You can reach out to us in several ways:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Direct reporting to any staff member</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Online confidential reporting form</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Parent contact with teachers/school leaders</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong><u>&nbsp;</u></strong>
</p>
<p><strong><u>How We Respond</u></strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Immediate physical and psychological safety measures for all students
involved</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Prompt investigation and timely updates, subject to case complexity</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Consequences, including disciplinary actions, conduct grade adjustments,
learning prosocial skills and reconciliation of relationships</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Support plans for affected students, including victims and offender</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Restorative support including school counselling, external referrals and
community resources</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Partnership with parents throughout the process, including informing parents
at the earliest opportunity</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong><u>How Parents Can Support</u></strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Talk regularly with your child about school experiences and friendships</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Teach respectful behaviour and reinforce healthy digital habits at home</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Monitor your child’s online and offline interactions and activities</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Share concerns promptly with your child’s Form Teacher or Year Head</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Work collaboratively with school on agreed interventions</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Model positive behaviour in your own interactions, such as being calm
and respectful in communications</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Use 'teachable moments' in your daily interactions with your child to
explain why certain conduct is inappropriate</p>
</li>
</ul>
